# Product Requirements

## Overview
Clyra is a healthcare claims operations assistant designed for U.S. payer, provider, and revenue-cycle teams. The product supports deterministic claim rule checks, AI-assisted recommendations, and human-reviewed approvals before any consequential automation executes.

## Core principles
- AI recommends; the human decides.
- Consequential actions require explicit human approval.
- All demo data is synthetic and privacy-safe.
